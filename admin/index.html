How to connect your Office TallyPrime in 2 Minutes:
  1. Open TallyPrime on your PC → Press F1 (Help) → Settings → Connectivity → Set "TallyPrime acts as" = Both, Port = 9000.
  2. Click the black "Download Local PC Connector (.zip)" button above, unzip it on your desktop, and double-click START-TALLY-SYNC.bat.
  3. That's it! As long as the window is open, any invoice synced from this panel posts directly into your Tally DayBook & Ledger without manual typing!
